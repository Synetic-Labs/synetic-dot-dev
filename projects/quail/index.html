Project Quail

An FPV drone for autonomy research

■ About

Development of an FPV drone that is capable of agile flight while being the ideal platform for research on autonomous drones. Born from our own need for this hardware, we are developing a custom drone dedicated to research: safe to fly indoors, expandable with sensors, and capable of running an autonomous flight controller. The goal is to make it a successful, fully open source, digital-video-capable hardware product.
